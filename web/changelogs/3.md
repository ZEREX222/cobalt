here are the latest updates for cobalt.canine.tools!

* medal.tv clips are now supported. you cannot pick the quality as of now, but it works (thanks to [hyperdefined](https://hyper.lol)).
* soundcloud downloading is currently wonky, going to be fixed in the future (thanks to [patrick](https://patriick.dev/)).

please be patient with us! we are hobbyist developers who are maintaining cobalt. the official [cobalt.tools](https://cobalt.tools) website is abandoned, so people like [hyperdefined](https://hyper.lol) and [patrick](https://patriick.dev/) are working on improvements as time flows.

it's unfortunate that the developers have abandoned it, but thankfully community members are here to save the day! if you want to contribute, please do so [here](https://github.com/zImPatrick/cobalt)!

if you want to help cobalt, spread the word about community instances! please see [cobalt.directory](https://cobalt.directory/) for information.

happy barking!
